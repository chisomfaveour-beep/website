About NaijaScholar

Why this exists, and who built it.

🇳🇬

The Problem

Millions of talented Nigerian students miss out on scholarships every year — not because they aren't qualified, but because they simply didn't know the opportunity existed. Deadlines pass. Applications are missed. Dreams are delayed.

💡

The Solution

NaijaScholar is a free, easy-to-use platform that lists scholarships available to Nigerian students — from local government funding to full international scholarships at MIT, Harvard, Oxford, and beyond. No signup required. No fees. Ever.

🚀

The Vision

This started as a personal project — a Nigerian student who wanted to invent, create, and help others. The goal is to grow NaijaScholar into the #1 scholarship resource for all of Africa, empowering the next generation of African leaders, scientists, and innovators.

🤝

How You Can Help

Know a scholarship that isn't listed here? Submit it and help another student. Share NaijaScholar with friends, classmates, and teachers. Together, we can make sure no Nigerian student misses an opportunity.
